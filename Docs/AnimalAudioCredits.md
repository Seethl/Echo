# Recordings used by the manor vinyl puzzle

The same processed AudioClip assets are used by the three vinyl buttons and the radio transmission.
These recordings have separate provenance from the game's source code and visual assets.

| Asset | Author/source | Source page and license | Processing |
|---|---|---|---|
| WolfHowl.wav | U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service | [Wolf howls](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wolf_howls.ogg), public domain, PD-USGov-FWS | Opening 3.2 seconds; converted to mono 22,050 Hz PCM; short edge fades and peak matching. |
| MonkeyCall.wav | AntumDeluge | [Monkey Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/monkey-sounds), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) | Combines monkey-1, monkey-2, monkey-3 with 0.14-second gaps; mono 22,050 Hz PCM; short fades and peak matching. The author describes these recordings as a human vocal imitation of monkey calls. |
| BirdChirp.wav | syncopika | [Bird chirping sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/bird-chirping-sounds), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) | Excerpt 0.30–2.85 seconds; mono 22,050 Hz PCM; short fades and peak matching. Original recorded in the author's backyard. |

No pitch shifting or time stretching was used. The processed files peak at approximately 0.70; runtime matching preserves the same waveform for the radio and the associated vinyl record.
The original recordings are not bundled in their full length. The relevant source pages above provide the originals and licensing information.
