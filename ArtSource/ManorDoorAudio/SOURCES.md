# Recorded wooden doors and cupboards

All three recordings are CC0. Public HQ MP3 previews were downloaded without an account; they are retained unchanged here. Game WAVs are edited mono 22050 Hz PCM excerpts, not generated tones.

- soundofsong, **door creak 1.wav**, https://freesound.org/people/soundofsong/sounds/647646/ — `DoorCreak-original.mp3`; opening creak excerpt 0.02–0.92 s.
- ApeinCave, **Kitchen cupboard door creak open**, https://freesound.org/people/ApeinCave/sounds/855584/ — `CupboardOpen-original.mp3`; cupboard opening excerpt 0.15–1.30 s.
- ApeinCave, **Kitchen cupboard door creak close**, https://freesound.org/people/ApeinCave/sounds/855583/ — `CupboardClose-original.mp3`; door movement 1.55–2.25 s, cupboard movement 2.65–3.45 s, door latch 4.96–5.50 s, cupboard latch 4.98–5.32 s.

License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Authors and original sound pages verified at download. Processing: 100–6000 Hz bandpass, level matching with peak headroom, 8 ms edge fades. A single source is reused within a door; movement is replaced on reversal and the closing impact is triggered only at the closed position. Gameplay hearing remains separate from playback.
